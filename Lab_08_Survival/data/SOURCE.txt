File: veteran.csv (columns: trt, celltype, time, status, karno, diagtime, age, prior)

Source: the dataset veteran in the R package survival (version 3.8-9; R 4.6).
Veterans' Administration Lung Cancer study: a randomised trial of two treatment
regimens (standard and test chemotherapy) for advanced inoperable lung cancer.
137 patients, 128 deaths and 9 censored survival times.

Variables:
  trt       treatment: standard, test (coded 1, 2 in the package)
  celltype  tumour cell type: squamous, smallcell, adeno, large
  time      survival time in days from randomisation
  status    1 = death observed, 0 = censored
  karno     Karnofsky performance score (10-99; higher = better general condition)
  diagtime  months from diagnosis to randomisation
  age       age in years
  prior     prior therapy: no, yes (coded 0, 10 in the package)

The file was written with
  library(survival)
  v <- survival::veteran
  v$trt <- factor(v$trt, 1:2, c("standard", "test"))
  v$prior <- factor(v$prior, c(0, 10), c("no", "yes"))
  v$celltype <- as.character(v$celltype)
  write.csv(v[, c("trt", "celltype", "time", "status", "karno", "diagtime", "age", "prior")],
            "data/veteran.csv", row.names = FALSE)
In R, set the reference level when reading:
  factor(celltype, levels = c("squamous", "smallcell", "adeno", "large")).
