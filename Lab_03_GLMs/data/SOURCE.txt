birthwt.csv
  Source: R package 'MASS' (Venables and Ripley), version 7.3-66, object 'birthwt': risk factors
  associated with low infant birth weight; 189 births at Baystate Medical Center, Springfield,
  Massachusetts, during 1986.
  Original reference: Hosmer, D. W. and Lemeshow, S. (1989) Applied Logistic Regression. New York: Wiley.
  Also: Venables, W. N. and Ripley, B. D. (2002) Modern Applied Statistics with S. Fourth edition. Springer.
  Columns: low (1 if birth weight < 2.5 kg), age (years), lwt (mother's weight at last menstrual period,
  pounds), race (white, black, other; coded 1, 2, 3 in MASS), smoke (smoking during pregnancy),
  ptl (number of previous premature labours), ht (history of hypertension), ui (uterine irritability),
  ftv (number of physician visits in the first trimester), bwt (birth weight, grams).
  Values unchanged except that race is written with its labels; written with
    bw <- MASS::birthwt
    bw$race <- factor(bw$race, levels = 1:3, labels = c("white", "black", "other"))
    write.csv(bw, "birthwt.csv", row.names = FALSE)
  License: MASS is distributed under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or 3 (GPL-2 | GPL-3).

ships.csv
  Source: R package 'MASS' (Venables and Ripley), version 7.3-66, object 'ships': data frame giving the
  number of damage incidents and aggregate months of service by ship type, year of construction,
  and period of operation.
  Original reference: McCullagh, P. and Nelder, J. A. (1989) Generalized Linear Models. Second edition.
  Chapman & Hall, Section 6.3.2.
  Columns: type (A-E), year (year of construction: 60 = 1960-64, 65 = 1965-69, 70 = 1970-74, 75 = 1975-79),
  period (period of operation: 60 = 1960-74, 75 = 1975-79), service (aggregate months of service),
  incidents (number of damage incidents). Six rows have service = 0; the lab drops them.
  Values unchanged; written with write.csv(MASS::ships, "ships.csv", row.names = FALSE).
  License: MASS is distributed under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or 3 (GPL-2 | GPL-3).
