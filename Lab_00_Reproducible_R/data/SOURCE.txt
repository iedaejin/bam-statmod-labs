File: nhtemp.csv (columns: year, temp)

Source: the dataset nhtemp in the R package datasets (part of R; R 4.6.1).
Average yearly temperatures in New Haven: the mean annual temperature in degrees
Fahrenheit in New Haven, Connecticut, from 1912 to 1971. A time series of 60 observations.
The file was written with
  data(nhtemp)
  write.csv(data.frame(year = as.numeric(time(nhtemp)), temp = as.numeric(nhtemp)),
            "data/nhtemp.csv", row.names = FALSE)

Original source, as given in ?nhtemp:

  <https://cycles.org/cycles/library/>.
