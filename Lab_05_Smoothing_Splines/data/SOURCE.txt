mcycle.csv

Source: R package MASS (Venables and Ripley), data set `mcycle`, written with
  write.csv(MASS::mcycle, "data/mcycle.csv", row.names = FALSE)
Columns: times = time after a simulated motorcycle impact (milliseconds);
         accel = head acceleration of the crash-test dummy (g).
133 rows; the rows are sorted by time and there are only 94 distinct times.

Original reference: Silverman, B. W. (1985). Some aspects of the spline smoothing
approach to non-parametric curve fitting. Journal of the Royal Statistical Society,
Series B, 47, 1-52. Also described in Venables, W. N. and Ripley, B. D. (2002),
Modern Applied Statistics with S, 4th ed., Springer.
